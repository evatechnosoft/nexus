# Evaitec Logo Redesign Specifications

Detailed requirements for the next iteration of the Evaitec logo.

## Typography
- **E**: Styled as a reversed/mirrored number **3**.
- **v**: Styled as an asymmetric **checkmark** (short left arm, long right arm extending slightly over the next characters).
- **ai / @i**: Both letters must be contained **INSIDE** a single, prominent **integrated circuit (microchip)** icon. The 'i' should not be outside the chip.
- **tec**: The letters 't', 'e', and 'c' should incorporate **circuit board trace patterns** and nodes into their strokes to emphasize the technology aspect.
- **Unity**: All letters should feel like a single, cohesive wordmark with consistent weight.

## Iconography (Above Wordmark)
- **Infinity Symbol**: An infinity loop positioned above the text.
- **Left Loop**: Minimalist and sparse **ivy/vine** with a few small, elegant leaves.
- **Right Loop**: Sleek **digital circuit board traces** with glowing connection nodes.
- **Transition**: A smooth, seamless morph from organic vine to digital circuit at the center crossing point.

## Color & Style
- **Gradient**: Smooth transition from **light fresh green** (left) to **aqua turquoise** (middle) to **light sky blue** (right).
- **Glassmorphism**: Use frosted glass translucent effects, soft light diffusion, and subtle luminous glows.
- **No Subtitles**: Remove the "YAZILIM VE YAPAY ZEKA TEKNOLOJİLERİ" text.
- **Background**: Pure white.

## Reference Styles
- Maintain the premium, 3D/glassmorphic look of the original concept while simplifying the organic elements.
